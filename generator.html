Funnel

Builds a level set stage by stage. Every level draws its own profile from its stage, is simulated with the tempo model below, and is kept only when it meets the stage's targets (knots, chains, decoys, extras, easy opening and ending). No target word is used twice in the whole set. Levels that miss a target after all tries are kept anyway and marked in the Status column.
Stages (JSON)
One stage per line, covering levels from–to. words/layers ranges, top = words on the top layer, knots range, knotSingleMax = knots with a single linkable word, reversed = max share reading left/up, decoy = max decoys / near misses at easy and knot steps, maxBonus, maxThreeLetter, maxStartNoise, loose/looseKnot = chance of L on a layer / knot layer, hardEnding = chance of a harder last step, opening/ending = max cost of the first 3 / last 2 steps as a multiple of the median, chainAfterKnot = steps of the chain expected after the knot, lenBias = weight toward long words. A fixed profile string replaces the random one. A stage may give sizes (list of [W, H], one drawn per level) instead of W/H and tiers, cycled in order over the levels (level tierBase → first tier, the next → second, …); a tier overrides the stage's fields and may use wordsBySize ({"5x5": [10, 10], …}) or density (words per cell) instead of words, clamped to minWords–maxWords, and bonusPerWord instead of maxBonus. fullBoard = every cell gets a letter (placement pulls words onto open cells, levels with gaps are rejected), reversedBias = backwards words lean toward deep layers (weight (layer+1)^bias; 0 = anywhere), reversedCenter = min mean solve position of backwards words, knotReversed = chance a knot layer is forced backwards. maxExtra4 / maxExtra3 = max extras any solve order can reveal, independent 4+ letter ones (firm) and 3-letter ones (soft); stems/extensions of a target are never counted.
Tempo model
A simulated player who knows the word list finds, at every step, the cheapest linkable word. Word cost = (1 + dist × cells from the last find / distScale) × reversed (if it reads left/up) × (1 + crowd × other visible tiles with its first letter); a forward word that was already ≥ preVis visible and near the last find (≤ preVisCells) or visible at the start costs × preVisFactor. Step cost = cheapest word ÷ √(linkable words) + decoy per decoy + nearMiss per one-letter-short decoy. Knot: step ≥ knotMul × median. Chain: chainMin+ steps ≤ chainMul × median, each within chainDist cells of the previous find.
